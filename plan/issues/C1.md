## Context

PLAN.md is the verified contract; DESIGN.md is the locked visual spec. This item turns them into the durable spec documents the rest of the build cites: a product requirements doc (`PRD.md`), an architecture doc (`ARCHITECTURE.md`), and architecture decision records under `docs/adr/` for every decision that is not already covered by ADR-0001/0002.

Follow the ADR-0001 format (status, context, decision, consequences).
- Cross-link: PRD ↔ ARCHITECTURE ↔ ADRs ↔ `plan/dag.yaml`.
